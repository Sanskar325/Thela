Our promise

A clean cart, fresh oil, and food cooked in front of you.

  • Fresh oil every evening

    We start each evening with new oil. Nothing is fried in yesterday's.

  • Cooked when you order

    Nothing sits waiting under a cloth. Your plate goes straight from the tawa to your hands.

  • Clean hands, filtered water

    Gloves and caps at the counter, and RO water for the chutneys and the chai.
