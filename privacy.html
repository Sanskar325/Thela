Ghoom XI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Ghoom XI is a fast food cart at Line Bazar Chowk, Purnia, Bihar 854301, run by Ritishek Verma and Sanskar Verma. This policy covers the Ghoom XI app for Android and iPhone and our website at thela-alpha.vercel.app.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. Your menu choices, your order and any coupon stay on your phone while you use the app, and are cleared when you close it.

When you send us an order or a message

When you tap Send on WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp with your message filled in: the dishes, the total, pickup or delivery details, and any coupon or note you added. Nothing is sent until you tap Send inside WhatsApp yourself.

Once you send it, we receive that message along with your WhatsApp number and name, as WhatsApp shows them to us. WhatsApp's own privacy policy covers how WhatsApp handles your messages.

What we do with it

  • Prepare your order, and deliver it if you asked for delivery.
  • Keep your Ghoom card: your WhatsApp number, your name, how many orders we have confirmed, your stamps, which of the four favourites you have tried, and which rewards you have used. We keep this in a private online record that only we can open.
  • Reply to your questions, feedback and party orders.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Other apps the app opens

The app can open WhatsApp, Google Maps for directions, and your UPI app to pay. Each of them has its own privacy policy.

Seeing or deleting your Ghoom card

Message us on WhatsApp to ask what we hold about you, or to delete it. We will delete your Ghoom card within 7 days of your request.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information about them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
